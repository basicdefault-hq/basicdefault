Senko Timer

A calm timer you'll come back to. Turn the ripple dial to set the time, give it a name, and it's one tap away next time. While it runs, a stick of incense slowly burns down.

  • Ripple dial. Set the time by feel, not by typing numbers.
  • Saved timers. Name the ones you use most and start them in one tap.
  • Steps and repeats. Chain steps like work and rest, and go round as many times as you like.
  • Made for every fold. A layout for closed, open and propped up like a tent, and for a regular iPhone too.
