Senko Timer

Privacy.

Short version: your timers stay on your phone. The app only counts a few things anonymously, and you can turn that off.

Support Privacy Terms

Draft. This policy is waiting on legal review and may change before Senko Timer is released.

What stays on your device

Your timers, their names, their steps and step names, and your settings are stored on your iPhone. Timer and step names never leave your device. There are no accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing about you held on a server.

What the app counts

To learn which parts of the app get used and what breaks, Senko Timer sends anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, an analytics service built for privacy. These are things like "a timer was started" or "the app was opened on a folded phone", along with basic details such as app version, iOS version and device model.

The counts are not tied to your name, email, Apple ID or anything else that identifies you. TelemetryDeck receives an anonymised identifier, not one that can be traced back to you or used across other apps.

What it never does

  • No accounts.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers or advertisers.

Turning it off

You can stop sending usage counts at any time in Settings › Share anonymous usage inside the app. The app works exactly the same either way.

Feedback you send

If you use the support form, what you write is used only to look into your note. Links from the app's Settings can attach the app version, build, iOS version, device model, fold posture and theme to help reproduce a bug; you can see these on the form before sending.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
