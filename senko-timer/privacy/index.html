Privacy.

Your timers stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. The app counts a few things anonymously, and you can turn that off.

This policy explains how basicDEFAULT ("we", "us") handles information when you use Senko Timer (the "app"). We built the app to collect as little as possible.

At a glance

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • Your timers, steps and settings stay on your device. Timer and step names never leave it.
  • Anonymous usage counts help us improve the app. You can turn them off.
  • If you use the rock garden, your approximate location is sent to Apple to get the local weather. We never receive it.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The timers you save, their names, their steps and your settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them. They may be included in your device's own backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage data

The app sends anonymous usage counts through TelemetryDeck, an analytics service designed for privacy. These are signals such as "a timer was started" or "the app was opened on a folded phone", together with basic technical details like app version, iOS version and device model.

This data is not linked to you. It doesn't include your name, email, Apple ID, location or the contents of your timers. TelemetryDeck receives an anonymised identifier that can't be traced back to you and isn't used across other apps. We use the counts only in aggregate, to understand which features are used and to find problems.

Location and weather

The rock garden, part of Senko Plus, can show the weather where you are. To do that, the app asks for permission to use your approximate location and sends it to Apple's WeatherKit service, which returns the current conditions. This happens at most about every 30 minutes, and only while the garden is showing.

Your location goes only to Apple for this. It isn't sent to us, to TelemetryDeck or to anyone else, and it isn't stored by the app. The app only asks when you choose the garden or automatic weather in Settings. If you say no, the garden still works with clear weather.

Purchases

Senko Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your payment details or Apple ID. The app only checks with Apple whether you have Plus.

Feedback you send us

If you contact us through the support page, we receive what you write. Links from the app's Settings can also include the app version, build, iOS version, device model, fold posture and theme, so we can reproduce a problem. You can see these details before you send. We use your message only to respond to it and to improve the app.

How we share information

We don't sell, rent or share your information for advertising. We use a small number of service providers to run the app, such as TelemetryDeck for usage counts, and Apple for weather, purchases and App Store distribution. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Usage counts are kept in aggregate for as long as they're useful for improving the app. Feedback is kept for as long as we need it to deal with your message.

Your choices

  • Turn off usage counts at any time in Settings › Share anonymous usage. The app works the same either way.
  • Turn off location for Senko Timer at any time in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, or to object to how it's used. Because we don't collect information that identifies you, there is usually nothing for us to look up. If you have a request, reach us through the support page.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the date below. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
